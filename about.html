who i am

the story so far

i'm khang, a musician and producer working across hip hop, indie, and experimental spaces. my sound lives in the space between chill and melancholic—introspective lyricism paired with moody, atmospheric production.

vietnamese roots, global perspective. i think of my music as visual and emotional— it's meant to make you feel something specific. love songs with depth. sadness that doesn't hurt. the aesthetic of late nights and quiet reflection.

i engineer, produce, write, and rap. i'm interested in the full creative process—from initial idea to final mix. every decision matters. every sound has purpose.

inspired by: joji's atmospheric approach, hip hop's lyrical depth, indie's authenticity. i'm trying to build something that feels true to who i am.

how i work

authenticity over perfection. i believe the best music comes from real emotion and real experience. my approach is to capture that feeling and translate it into sound.

every project starts with a feeling—a moment, a memory, a concept. i work to translate that into lyrics, melodies, and production that honors the original emotion.

i'm obsessed with detail: the exact synth pad, the way a vocal sits in the mix, the space between beats. these details accumulate into an atmosphere, a vibe, a world.

open to collaborations with artists, producers, and creatives who understand that vision matters more than hype.
